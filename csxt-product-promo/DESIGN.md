# AI 参数系统 20 秒产品宣传片设计

## Tool Summary

AI 参数系统的宣传片只展示前台工具本身：报价单上传、产品确认、参数匹配、人工精修、AI 改写、格式整理和 Word 导出。画面重点是让观众看见工具怎么工作。

## Colors

- Deep canvas: `#050B13`
- Panel navy: `#071827`
- Cyan accent: `#52E7F2`
- Mint action: `#57E6B6`
- Warm amber: `#FFC565`
- App blue: `#2F6FD8`
- Paper surface: `#F7FAFE`
- Text white: `#F8FBFF`
- Muted text: `#A9B8CC`

## Typography

- Primary UI/video font: `Inter`, sans-serif.
- Headline weight: 800-900 with tight, stable blocks.
- Body and labels: 22px or larger for video readability.

## Motion Rules

- Motion should feel like a controlled workflow: scanning lines, panel slides, radial pulses, document cards moving down a production lane.
- Use clean crossfades and wipe transitions between scenes.
- Screenshots should never sit flat; use device frames, subtle zoom, tilt, and parallax layers.
- Numeric proof points should count or pop into place.

## What Not To Do

- Do not use generic purple SaaS gradients.
- Do not make the app look like a chatbot.
- Do not overcrowd frames with full feature lists.
- Do not use live iframes; captured screenshots are the source material.
- Do not show any non-tool surfaces or operations.
- Do not introduce colors outside the palette unless used as subtle alpha variants.
